Return & Refund Policy

⏳ Rental Duration

The outfit must be returned within the agreed/stipulated time.

We know you'll absolutely slay in these outfits and avoid attracting any late-penalty charges. 😉

Late returns will attract additional charges. The penalty may vary depending on the value of the outfit and the duration of the delay.

❌ Cancellation Policy

We kindly request you to avoid last-minute cancellations and unnecessary inconvenience.

However, there will be no cancellation charges if you inform us at least 1 hour in advance.

💰 Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit may be required.

If applicable, the amount will be informed before booking.

The deposit will be returned after the outfit has been received and inspected.

📦 Return Condition

The outfit must be returned in the same condition in which it was received, subject to normal wear associated with the rental.

Any stains, tears, burns, missing accessories, or other damage may result in charges based on the extent of damage, repair or replacement cost, and original value of the outfit.
