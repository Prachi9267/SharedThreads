Terms of Services

🤍 Before You Read the Terms & Conditions…

We trust our girls. 💖

We know that you'll rent these outfits with enthusiasm and excitement, and we also know how responsible, careful, and beautiful you are. We trust that you'll return the outfit in the same condition with the same love and care with which you received it.

These guidelines have been thoughtfully put together to ensure a smooth rental experience, strengthen mutual trust, and avoid any misunderstandings or discrepancies in the future.

🧼 Hygiene & Cleanliness

Our team does not compromise on either the quality or cleanliness of the outfits.

The outfits are properly washed, cleaned, and sanitized before and after every use to give you the best dress-rental experience. 😉

As ensuring hygiene and cleanliness is our utmost priority, we kindly request you not to wash or dry-clean the outfit after use. Simply return it to us, and we'll take care of the rest.

🧺 Dry Cleaning

Sarees, lehengas, kurtis, and other ethnic outfits that add an extra glow to you will be dry-cleaned whenever required.

📦 Responsibility After Delivery

Once the outfit is handed over, we pass on the responsibility of maintaining its quality to you and trust you to return it safely.

Because sometimes, great outfits come with great responsibility. 🤭

a) Alterations

The waist, chest, bust, and other necessary measurements of every outfit will be clearly mentioned on the website alongside the outfit photographs.

We kindly request you to go through these measurements carefully before placing an order, as alteration requests or size-related issues after delivery will not be entertained.

b) Damage Policy

Any damage, including stains, tears, burns, or missing parts/accessories, will require compensation based on:

  • The extent of the damage
  • Repair or replacement cost
  • The original value of the outfit

A video of every outfit will be recorded before handover and again at the time of return to avoid any future disagreements regarding its condition.

If there are any pre-existing defects or minor damages, they will be clearly mentioned on the website itself, and the decision to proceed with the rental will be entirely yours.

All accessories provided with the outfit, such as dupattas, belts, jackets, or any additional items, must be returned along with it. Missing accessories may attract additional charges.

In case an outfit is lost or cannot be returned, compensation up to the replacement value of the outfit may be applicable.

📅 Booking Confirmation

Outfits will be allotted on a first-come, first-served basis.

A booking will be considered confirmed only after receiving confirmation from our team.

🪪 Verification (College ID)

A valid College ID must be shown at the time of delivery or pickup before renting an outfit.

Basic details from your ID will be noted only for verification purposes and will be kept confidential. They will not be shared with any third party.

❌ Cancellation Policy

We kindly request you to avoid last-minute cancellations and unnecessary inconvenience.

However, there will be no cancellation charges if you inform us at least 1 hour in advance.

💰 Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit may be required.

If applicable, the amount will be informed before booking.

The deposit will be returned after the outfit has been received and inspected.

⚠️ Right to Refuse

In exceptional circumstances, we reserve the right to decline or cancel a rental request whenever necessary.
