Our Story

This idea was born during a casual conversation in our PG, when Vishakha randomly suggested it. What started as a simple discussion soon turned into a solution that addresses both affordability and sustainability.By encouraging people to rent outfits instead of buying new ones, we aim to reduce dependence on fast fashion while giving everyone the freedom to wear something different without worrying about repeating outfits.

Our goal is to make fashion more accessible, budget-friendly, and environmentally conscious—proving that looking great doesn't have to come at the cost of your wallet or the planet.
